MIDFIELD DEVELOPMENT SESSION

Midfield Turning + Ball Distribution

A technical midfield session focused on receiving shape, turning into space, clean ball distribution and explosive movement.

SCAN EARLY → OPEN UP → TURN INTO SPACE → DISTRIBUTE WITH QUALITY → EXPLODE
Focus Receiving, Turning, Passing
Players 1-2 Players
Equipment Ladder, Cones, Balls

Session Standards

  • Check shoulder before receiving.
  • Open the body before the ball arrives.
  • First touch should have purpose.
  • Accelerate after the turn.
  • Pass with pace and accuracy.

Warm-Up 1 - Agility Ladder + Side Shuffle + Sprint

Complete the ladder pattern, exit laterally, then accelerate. Each ladder variation is completed once to the right and once to the left.

WARM-UP

Ladder → Side Shuffle → Sprint

4 Ladder Patterns
2 Directions
8 Total Sets

How It Works

  1. Complete the selected agility ladder pattern.
  2. Exit the ladder under control.
  3. Rep 1 - side shuffle to the right.
  4. Rep 2 - side shuffle to the left.
  5. Finish each rep with a short acceleration.
  6. Use 4 different ladder patterns.
Player Cue Quick feet → stay balanced → shuffle → accelerate.

Activity 2A - Half Turn + Dribble Into Space + Turn + Pass Back

The midfielder receives centrally, opens on the half turn, drives into space and then turns again before playing back to the passer.

MIDFIELD TURNING

Receive → Turn → Drive → Turn → Pass

5 Right Side
5 Left Side
10 Total Reps

Sequence

  1. Passer plays into the midfielder.
  2. Midfielder checks shoulder before receiving.
  3. Receive on the half turn.
  4. Take the ball wide and in front of the cone.
  5. Drive through the outside gate.
  6. Make a quick turn after passing through the gate.
  7. Play the ball firmly back to the passer.
  8. Hustle back to the central starting position.
  9. Repeat on the opposite side.

Turns To Work

Back Foot Receive and move immediately into open space.
Inside Across Body First touch across, second touch into space.
Trap / Step-Over Turn Sell one direction before exiting the other way.
Player Cue Scan → open body → first touch into space → drive.
4

Activity 2B - Back-Foot Receive + Drive + Turn + Pass Back

A simpler receiving pattern emphasizing body orientation, the back foot and a forward first touch.

RECEIVING SHAPE

Open Back Hip Toward the Target

Sequence

  1. Pass is played directly toward the midfielder.
  2. Check shoulder before the ball arrives.
  3. Open the back hip toward the target.
  4. Receive with the back foot.
  5. Use the first touch to move forward.
  6. Drive quickly through the gate.
  7. Turn and play the ball back to the passer.
Scan Early Know where the pressure and space are before receiving.
Open Up Do not receive square if you can receive facing forward.
Back Foot Receive with the foot furthest from the passer.
First Touch Forward The first touch should help you attack the space immediately.
Player Cue Check shoulder → open hip → back foot → drive.
5

Activity 3 - Low Driven Passing

Develop a firm, accurate midfield pass that travels quickly along the ground. Work both right and left foot.

RIGHT SIDE

Low Driven Pass

1. Plant Foot Place it beside the ball and point toward the target.
2. Chest Over Ball Keep the body over the strike so the ball stays down.
3. Locked Ankle Keep the striking foot firm through contact.
LEFT SIDE

Low Driven Pass

4. Middle of the Ball Strike through the center for a low driven pass.
5. Pass With Pace Ball should arrive firmly without bouncing.
6. Follow Through Finish the striking leg toward the target.
Passing Cue Plant beside it → chest over it → lock ankle → strike through the ball → follow the target.
6

Final Activity - 3 Full-Speed Sprints

Finish with three maximum-quality efforts. The goal is speed, not conditioning.

1

Sprint 1

Powerful first three steps.

2

Sprint 2

Aggressive arm action and forward acceleration.

3

Sprint 3

Finish through the line at maximum speed.

Forward Lean Accelerate with the whole body leaning forward from the ankles.
First 3 Steps Push hard into the ground instead of reaching forward.
Arm Drive Fast arms help create faster acceleration.
Full Recovery Take enough rest so all three sprints can be performed at full speed.
Final Cue Push → drive arms → accelerate → sprint through the finish.

Session Message

SCAN EARLY → OPEN UP → TURN INTO SPACE → DISTRIBUTE WITH QUALITY → EXPLODE

The goal is not simply to complete the drills. Every activity should reinforce the habits a midfielder needs in the game: awareness before receiving, body position, purposeful first touch, quick movement into space and accurate distribution.

Controlling Your Body Lean
• For Flat (Low-Driven): Keep your chest and head over the ball. Leaning your upper body forward forces your striking leg to finish lower, keeping the ball pinned to the grass.
• For Height (Lofted): Lean your torso back slightly right as you make contact. This opens up your hips and allows your leg to swing upward, creating an ascending launch angle.


2. Positioning Your Plant Foot
• For Flat (Low-Driven): Step directly next to the ball (roughly 5–6 inches to the side) with your toes pointing at your target. Landing even with the ball naturally keeps your weight centered over it.
• For Height (Lofted): Plant your non-kicking foot slightly behind and wider from the ball. This extra distance gives your kicking foot the physical space it needs to scoop underneath the ball.

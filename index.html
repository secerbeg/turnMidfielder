2A
RECEIVING + TURNING

Half Turn + Dribble Into Space + Turn + Pass Back

Scan, receive on the half turn, take the ball into space and distribute back with quality.

SEQUENCE

How the Activity Works

1 Passer serves the ball into the middle.
2 Receiver checks in, scans and opens on the half turn.
3 Take the ball wide in front of the side cone.
4 Accelerate and drive through the top gate.
5 Turn quickly and play back to the passer.
6 Hustle back to the starting position.
TURNS TO WORK

Three Receiving / Turning Solutions

1 Back foot into space.
2 Inside foot across the body, second touch out.
3 Trap / step-over turn.
Volume 5 reps right + 5 reps left, then break.

Coaching Focus

✓ Check shoulder before receiving.
✓ Open the body early.
✓ First touch takes you into space.
✓ Take the ball in front of the side cone.
✓ Attack the gate with speed.
✓ Turn quickly and pass back cleanly.
Player Cue Scan → open → first touch into space → accelerate → turn → pass.
2B
SIMPLER PROGRESSION

Back-Foot Receive + Drive + Turn + Pass Back

Remove the central movement and focus directly on receiving wide with the correct body shape.

SEQUENCE

Receive and Go

1 Passer plays directly to the wide receiver.
2 Check shoulder before the ball arrives.
3 Open the back hip toward the target.
4 Receive on the back foot.
5 First touch goes forward into space.
6 Drive through the gate, turn and pass back.
BODY SHAPE

Open the Back Hip

Do not receive square to the passer.

As the pass travels, open the back hip toward the direction you want to attack.

The first touch should immediately move the ball forward rather than stopping it underneath you.

Volume 5 reps each side, then break.

Coaching Focus

✓ Check shoulder before the ball arrives.
✓ Open the back hip toward the target.
✓ Receive on the back foot.
✓ First touch goes forward.
✓ Drive through the gate with speed.
✓ Turn and pass back cleanly.
Player Cue Check → open → back foot → touch forward → drive.
3
DISTRIBUTION

Low Driven Passing

Develop a firm, accurate pass that travels quickly along the ground with clean technique on both feet.

CONTACT POINT

Which Part of the Foot Makes Contact?

Use the middle-inside / instep area of the foot to create a firm passing surface.

Turn the striking foot slightly outward, lock the ankle and present a strong surface toward the intended target.

For the low-driven pass, connect through the middle of the ball rather than getting underneath it.

Contact Cue Face the target → lock the ankle → connect cleanly → strike through the middle.
BODY POSITION

Controlling Your Body Lean

FLAT / LOW-DRIVEN

Chest and Head Over the Ball

Keep your chest and head over the ball.

Leaning your upper body forward helps the striking leg finish lower, keeping the ball driven along the grass.

Chest over ball → strike through → low follow-through.
HEIGHT / LOFTED

Slight Lean Back

Lean the torso back slightly as you make contact.

This gives the striking leg room to travel upward and creates a higher launch angle.

Slight lean back → get underneath → upward follow-through.
SUPPORT FOOT

Positioning Your Plant Foot

FLAT / LOW-DRIVEN

Beside the Ball

Step directly next to the ball - approximately 5-6 inches to the side.

Point the toes of the plant foot toward the target and plant roughly even with the ball.

This helps keep your weight centered over the strike.

Beside the ball → toes to target → weight over the ball.
HEIGHT / LOFTED

Slightly Behind and Wider

Plant the non-kicking foot slightly behind and wider from the ball.

The extra distance gives the kicking foot more room to travel underneath the ball.

Slightly behind → slightly wider → create room underneath.
RIGHT FOOT

Low Driven Pass - Right

1. Approach Approach at a slight angle and stay balanced.
2. Plant Foot Place the support foot beside the ball with the toes toward the target.
3. Body Lean Keep the chest and head over the ball.
4. Contact Lock the ankle and strike through the middle of the ball.
5. Follow Through Continue the striking leg toward the target.
LEFT FOOT

Low Driven Pass - Left

1. Match the Technique Reproduce the same body shape used on the right side.
2. Plant Foot Plant beside the ball and point toward the intended target.
3. Locked Ankle Keep the striking foot firm through contact.
4. Strike Through the Middle Do not get underneath the ball when the goal is a low-driven pass.
5. Pass With Pace The ball should travel firmly along the ground with minimal bounce.
TECHNIQUE CHECK

Low-Driven Pass - 6 Keys

1 Plant foot beside the ball.
2 Plant foot points toward target.
3 Chest and head stay over ball.
4 Lock the striking ankle.
5 Strike through middle of ball.
6 Follow through toward target.
Passing Cue Plant beside it → chest over it → lock the ankle → strike through the middle → follow through to the target.

Take These Habits Into the Game
